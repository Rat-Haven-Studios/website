Community Resource Hub

by hutner Resource Posted 09/10/2026 • Updated 09/14/2026

Overview

This is a collection of tools, links, and resources we've found useful and want to share! Whenever we find a new resource that we want to share, we'll put it here.

Overall, this doesn't really go into depth on any resource, but is more of a hub/link collection. For more in-depth resource articles, check out the "Resource" tag of the workshop!

Dev Tools

Tool Description
Godot Engine The engine we use to make our games.
Aseprite Pixel art software. We use it to make pixel art for our games.
Obsidian A note-taking and knowledge management tool. We use it to organize our ideas and research.
FL Studio A digital audio workstation. We use it to make music for our games.
