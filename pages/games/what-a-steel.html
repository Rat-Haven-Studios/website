What a STEEL!

Game Jam • Roguelike • Aug 30, 2026

You're a cyborg robot fighter struggling to keep up with the times, and the only augments you can get come from sketchy dealers. Take what they tell you with a grain of salt, and figure out what you're really buying.

Made for Brackeys Game Jam 2026.2 under the theme "Trust No One".

How to Play

Controls

  • WASD / Arrow Keys - move
  • Z / LMB - attack
  • E - interact
  • Space - dodge
  • I - open journal
  • Full controller support

Goal

Power up and kill your way through waves of enemies. Between waves, spend what you earned at the shop, but the shopkeepers lie about what their items actually do.

You only find out what something really does once you buy it and see its effect in your Journal. Build up your understanding of what everything actually does, since you can't trust a word the shopkeepers say.
