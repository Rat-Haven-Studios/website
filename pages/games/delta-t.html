Delta T

Game Jam • Story Puzzle • July 26, 2026

Aliens are invading the world and you have 2 minutes to stop them. Is that enough time to save the world?

Made for GMTK 2026 under the theme "Countdown".

How to Play

Controls

  • WASD / Arrow Keys - move
  • E - interact
  • LMB - Advance dialogue
  • R - reset

Goal

You have 2 minutes to stop the alien invasion! Go around the map and interact with your the people around to gather clues. Ask questions and figure out a way to stop the aliens before time runs out. Or else...

There are 4 different endings. Good luck!
