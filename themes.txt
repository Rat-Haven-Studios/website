Theme presets for styles/styles.css :root block
==================================================
To switch themes, copy the variable lines for a theme below and paste them
over the corresponding lines inside :root { ... } in styles/styles.css
(lines 6-20 as of this writing).

--------------------------------------------------
[original] - the theme the site shipped with before any tweaks
--------------------------------------------------
--bg: #06090f;
--surface: #0e1520;
--surface-2: #162030;
--border: #243c5e;
--accent: #4d7cfe;
--accent-dark: color-mix(in srgb, var(--accent) 75%, black);
--text: #eef4ff;
--text-muted: #96b8d8;
--bg-pattern: #0d1828;
(body::before had no opacity override - pattern at full strength)
